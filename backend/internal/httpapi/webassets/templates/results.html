Read the interval, not the rank. Each bar is the {{.Summary.Confidence}}% interval for that project, obtained by resampling judges with replacement and re-running the whole pipeline. {{.SeparableCount}} of {{len .Summary.Projects}} projects are separated from the project ranked immediately above them.

Separation is a statement about one adjacent pair. It is not a clique: a chain of overlapping neighbours can still contain a project that is clearly ahead of the bottom of the table. The portal will not claim a project beat another where their intervals overlap, because on this data it does not know. The order is still deterministic and complete; what is missing is evidence for the differences.
